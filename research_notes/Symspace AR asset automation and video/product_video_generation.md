## KQ1. Open-weight video models: state on 2026-10-07 (licenses, size, VRAM, speed, rankings)

### Takeaway
As of October 2026 the open-weight field for a commercial, Western-deployable product-video pipeline narrows to four practical families: **Wan 2.2** (Apache-2.0, July 2025; the last open Wan — 2.5/2.6 are API-only), **LTX-2 / 2.3 / 2.5** (Lightricks, Jan/Mar/Aug 2026; 19–22B, native audio, 4K; free under US$10M annual revenue, paid licence above), **HunyuanVideo 1.5** (Tencent, Nov 2025, 8.3B, cheapest to run; but licence excludes EU/UK/South Korea), and the very new **MAGI-2 Preview** (Sand.ai, Aug 2026, 114B MoE, Apache-2.0, heavy). **MiniMax H3** (Aug 2026, 33B, audio+video) ranks near the top of the Artificial Analysis arena but its community licence reportedly bars local deployment in the US/EU/UK/KR, so it is not usable for Symspace. Alibaba, Tencent and Lightricks all still publish weights; closed models (Seedance 2.0, Veo 3, Sora 2, Kling) remain ahead on human-preference arenas by roughly 150–250 Elo over the best deployable open models.

### Inferences
- For a company that must deploy in/for the EU or UK (Symspace's clients could be anywhere), only Wan 2.2 (Apache-2.0), LTX-2.x (community licence; free while Symspace revenue < US$10M), SkyReels (MIT/Skywork), MAGI-2 (Apache-2.0, impractically large), Open-Sora/CogVideoX/Mochi (older, weaker) are licence-clean. HunyuanVideo 1.5 and HunyuanCustom/Foley are off the table for EU/UK/KR use; MiniMax H3 is off the table for US/EU/UK/KR local deployment per secondary reports.
- Best quality-per-VRAM on a 24 GB card in Oct 2026: LTX-2.3/2.5 (FP8/FP4, 1080p 10 s in ~2–3 min on a 4090; native audio) or Wan2.2-TI2V-5B (5 s 720p in ~9 min, no audio). Wan2.2-A14B FP8/GGUF runs on 24 GB but slowly; its advantage is the VACE/Fun/Phantom/Uni3C control ecosystem (KQ2/3), which LTX lacks for 2.x.
- Human-preference gap between the best deployable open model (LTX-2.5 Pro ≈ 1,016 Elo) and closed leaders (Seedance 2.0 ≈ 1,191) is ~175 Elo — noticeable in cinematics, less relevant for a static product on a camera orbit.

## KQ2. Conditioning strategies for product fidelity (I2V vs render-to-video vs reference-driven vs 3D-consistent novel view)

### Takeaway
The only approach that guarantees geometry and logo fidelity is to **render the GLB deterministically** (Blender/Unity) and let the diffusion model add only what the render lacks (environment, lighting, "lifestyle" context) through structure-conditioned video-to-video (Wan VACE / Wan2.2-Fun-Control with depth + edge passes plus the render itself as reference). Pure image-to-video from the photo is cheapest but has no mechanism to hold a product's shape under rotation; reference/subject-driven models (Phantom, VACE R2V, SkyReels-V3 R2V, MAGREF, HunyuanCustom) were trained primarily for people and only partially for objects; 3D-aware novel-view models (Gen3C, Uni3C, ReCamMaster, Stable Virtual Camera) orbit a *scene* from a photo but are heavy, and SEVA is non-commercial. eBay's CVPR-2026 ConsID-Gen is the one product-specific academic system (multi-view-conditioned I2V on Wan2.1-Fun-1.3B) but its code/weights were still "under internal review" as of March 2026.

### Cited Findings

**Strategy 1 — Image-to-video from the product photo**
- All major open models offer I2V (Wan2.2 I2V-A14B / TI2V-5B, LTX-2.x, HunyuanVideo-1.5 I2V 480p/720p, SkyReels-V2, CogVideoX-1.5-I2V, FramePack). — [Wan2.2](https://github.com/Wan-Video/Wan2.2); [LTX-2](https://github.com/Lightricks/LTX-2); [HunyuanVideo-1.5](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/HunyuanVideo-1.5)
- Industry guidance (secondary): "Without real product reference images, fine details like logos and packaging text hallucinate, and shape drifts shot to shot"; recommended minimum reference set "front, 45°, side, and back views… a detail shot of hard-to-render elements like logos"; use a locked "visual anchor" still then animate; add text/logos "in post-production… motion graphics overlay". — [eachlabs orchestration guide 2026](https://www.eachlabs.ai/blog/how-to-orchestrate-text-to-image-to-video-workflows-in-2026) (snippet); [pctechmag Aug 2026](https://pctechmag.com/2026/08/from-face-drift-to-product-distortion-how-to-fix-ai-video-consistency/) (snippet)
- Amazon's closed reference: Amazon Ads' generator "turns one product image into a video clip a few seconds in length after just a few minutes of processing", free for Sponsored Brands advertisers, generating scenes from product details and reviews; 2025 update added multi-scene and longer formats. — [aboutamazon](https://aboutamazon.com/news/innovation-at-amazon/amazon-ads-generative-ai-video-generator-advertisers) (snippet)
- Google Shopping (closed reference) uses Veo to produce interactive 360° product views; earlier (2022) NeRF from five+ product photos. — [research.google](https://research.google/blog/bringing-3d-shoppable-products-online-with-generative-ai/) (snippet; page blocked)

**Strategy 2 — Render-to-video (deterministic GLB orbit + controlled V2V)**
- Wan2.1-VACE (ali-vilab; models May 2025, ICCV 2025): unified R2V (reference-to-video), V2V with "depth, pose, and other control signals", MV2V masked inpaint/outpaint, and compositions ("Move-Anything, Swap-Anything, Reference-Anything, Expand-Anything, Animate-Anything"); Wan2.1-VACE-1.3B (~81×480×832) and 14B (~81×720×1280) are Apache-2.0; VACE-LTX-Video-0.9 is RAIL-M. — [VACE GitHub](https://github.com/ali-vilab/VACE)
- Wan2.2 control models (Alibaba PAI VideoX-Fun, Apache-2.0): Wan2.2-Fun-Control (Canny, Depth, Pose, MLSD, trajectory; 512/768/1024; A14B 81 frames @16 fps, 5B 121 frames @24 fps) and Wan2.2-VACE-Fun-A14B (same controls + "subject-specific video generation via reference images", 81 frames @16 fps). — [VideoX-Fun GitHub](https://github.com/aigc-apps/VideoX-Fun); official ComfyUI template "Wan 2.2 14B Fun Control… guided by pose, depth, and edge inputs" — [comfy.org workflow](https://comfy.org/workflows/video_wan2_2_14B_fun_control-67a816af8a73/); Wan2.2-Fun-A14B-Control weights are "64.0 GB" on disk (BF16) — [gradually.ai](https://www.gradually.ai/en/ai-models/wan-2-2-a14b/) (snippet)
- Blender→ComfyUI pipeline exists as a packaged workflow: it "reads structure from depth, outline, or pose passes and uses that to drive Wan VACE video generation so your final footage stays aligned with the original animation intent", using Wan 2.1 VACE 14B and Depth Anything 3 for depth when the pass is not rendered. — [RunComfy "Blender to ComfyUI AI Renderer 2.0"](https://www.runcomfy.com/comfyui-workflows/blender-to-comfyui-ai-renderer-2-0-workflow-cinematic-video-output) (snippet; page blocked)
- Official ComfyUI VACE "Control Video" template: "guided video-to-video workflow that preserves the motion and timing of a source video while restyling… with a text prompt and a reference image". — [comfy.org VACE v2v](https://comfy.org/zh/workflows/video_wan_vace_14B_v2v-2652985596d8/) (snippet)
- LTX-Video 0.9.x had depth/pose/canny IC-LoRA control (8 Jul 2025) but LTX-2.x documents only generic IC-LoRA v2v, no depth/pose/canny. — [LTX-Video](https://github.com/Lightricks/LTX-Video); [LTX-2](https://github.com/Lightricks/LTX-2)
- Headless Blender: run `blender -b file.blend --python script.py`; turntable automation via bpy (camera constraint, rotate, render sequence); GLB import/export via `bpy.ops.export_scene.gltf`; the `blenderless` package wraps headless rendering ("batch generation of views from 3D files"). — [UArizona HPC Blender CLI](https://hpcdocs.hpc.arizona.edu/running_jobs/visualization/blender/command_line_rendering/); [blenderless on PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/blenderless/0.1.7/)

**Strategy 3 — Subject/reference-driven generation**

- MAGREF (ICLR 2026; code 16 Jun 2025): "any-reference video generation with subject disentanglement", masked guidance to avoid "copy-paste artifacts"; Wan2.1-based; Apache-2.0; "around 70 GB of VRAM, so an 80 GB GPU is recommended" (FP8 kijai nodes exist). — [MAGREF GitHub](https://github.com/MAGREF-Video/MAGREF)
- Stand-In (WeChatCV, 12 Aug 2025): identity preservation with 1% extra parameters (153M) on Wan2.1-14B-T2V; Apache-2.0; face-focused. — [Stand-In GitHub](https://github.com/WeChatCV/Stand-In)
- SkyReels-V3 Reference-to-Video (29 Jan 2026; 14B; 1–4 references incl. "object"; vendor "Reference Consistency 0.6698"). — [SkyReels-V3](https://github.com/SkyworkAI/SkyReels-V3)
- HunyuanCustom (8 May 2025): identity-consistent video from reference image + text/audio/video; Tencent Community licence (EU/UK/KR excluded). — [HunyuanCustom](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/HunyuanCustom)
- Wan2.2-S2V-14B (26 Aug 2025) is the open "presenter" option: audio-driven video from a reference image; Apache-2.0. — [Wan2.2](https://github.com/Wan-Video/Wan2.2)
- kijai's wrapper lists VACE, Phantom, ATI, Uni3C, ReCamMaster, MAGREF, EchoShot, WanAnimate, MultiTalk etc. under one ComfyUI node set, with FP8 and GGUF loaders and block-swap offloading ("512×512×81 frame generation used approximately 16GB with 20/40 blocks offloaded"). — [ComfyUI-WanVideoWrapper](https://github.com/kijai/ComfyUI-WanVideoWrapper)

**Strategy 4 — Camera-controlled / 3D-consistent novel-view generation**
- Uni3C (Alibaba; code 21 May 2025, weights 22 May 2025; Apache-2.0; Wan2.1-based): renders a point cloud from monocular depth and drives a "PCDController" for precise camera control (distance, elevation/azimuth, XYZ, focal; presets incl. "orbit"); single H20 ~50.8 GB with offload, ~20 min; 4×H20 FSDP <5 min. ComfyUI core has a `WanUni3CControlnetApply` node. — [Uni3C GitHub](https://github.com/ewrfcas/Uni3C); [ComfyUI node doc](https://docs.comfy.org/built-in-nodes/WanUni3CControlnetApply)
- ReCamMaster (Kuaishou; code 9 Apr 2025; **MIT**; open version on Wan2.1): re-renders a *source video* under 10 preset camera trajectories (pan/tilt/zoom/translate/arc), 81 frames @15 fps, 1280×1280 crop; authors warn the open Wan2.1 port "may not achieve the same results as demonstrated". — [ReCamMaster GitHub](https://github.com/KwaiVGI/ReCamMaster)
- GEN3C (NVIDIA, CVPR 2025; code 6 Jun 2025): Cosmos-7B-based, conditions on renders of a depth-derived 3D cache under user camera paths (incl. circular); source Apache-2.0, weights NVIDIA Open Model License; "~43GB" peak with full offloading on H100/A100. — [GEN3C GitHub](https://github.com/nv-tlabs/GEN3C)
- TrajectoryCrafter (ICCV 2025 oral; Feb–Mar 2025): monocular video → novel camera trajectory; "GPU with VRAM ≥ 28GB"; separate code and model licences (not read). — [TrajectoryCrafter GitHub](https://github.com/TrajectoryCrafter/TrajectoryCrafter)
- ViewCrafter (Sep 2024; Apache-2.0): single/sparse images + camera path → novel-view video; 25-frame model "23.5GB & 120s" on A100-40G. — [ViewCrafter GitHub](https://github.com/Drexubery/ViewCrafter)
- Stable Virtual Camera (Stability, Mar 2025 v1.0, Jun 2025 v1.1; 1.3B; 576p): **non-commercial licence; "The output follows the same non-commercial license"**. — [stable-virtual-camera GitHub](https://github.com/Stability-AI/stable-virtual-camera)
- SV4D 2.0 (May 2025): multi-view video diffusion for dynamic 3D assets from a single video; "available for both commercial and non-commercial use under the… Stability AI Community License". — [Stability news](https://stability.ai/news/stable-video-4d-20-new-upgrades-for-high-fidelity-novel-views-and-4d-generation-from-a-single-video) (snippet)

**Product-specific research**
- ConsID-Gen (eBay, CVPR 2026; arXiv 2602.10113): "product-centric scenarios where a single catalog photo must be turned into videos while preserving exact appearance"; augments the first frame with "unposed auxiliary views", dual-stream visual-geometric encoder, text-visual connector, DiT backbone; introduces ConsIDVid dataset and ConsIDVid-Bench; claims to surpass Wan2.1 and HunyuanVideo on identity fidelity. Base model Wan2.1-Fun-1.3B-InP; example prompt "A product-style close-up video with stable lighting and clean background". **Status: "We will release resources in stages after internal review" — dataset on HF; code and checkpoints "under internal review" as of March 2026.** — [ebay/consid-gen GitHub](https://github.com/ebay/consid-gen); [CVPR 2026 paper page](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Wu_ConsID-Gen_View-Consistent_and_Identity-Preserving_Image-to-Video_Generation_CVPR_2026_paper.html)
- DreamActor-H1 (ByteDance, arXiv 2506.10568): human-product demonstration videos from paired human + product images; masked cross-attention preserves "logos and textures"; 3D body mesh + product bounding boxes for motion guidance. No code release found. — [arXiv 2506.10568](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.10568v2) (abstract via search; page blocked)
- MagicMirror (ICCV 2025) is face-ID only. — [CVF](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/papers/Zhang_MagicMirror_ID-Preserved_Video_Generation_in_Video_Diffusion_Transformers_ICCV_2025_paper.pdf)
- Higgsfield (closed) markets "a single 2D image into a complete, cinematic 360-degree product visualization". — [Higgsfield blog](https://geo.higgsfield.ai/blog/higgsfield-ai-360-degree-product-views) (snippet)

### Inferences
- Identity preservation ordering (highest → lowest), inferred from mechanism: (a) deterministic render + depth/edge-conditioned V2V with the render as reference (geometry is pinned by the control signal every frame); (b) reference-driven S2V (Phantom/VACE R2V/SkyReels-V3) — good for silhouette and colour, unreliable for small text; (c) plain I2V from a photo — first frame perfect, drift grows with rotation; (d) 3D-aware novel-view models from a single photo — plausible but hallucinated back sides. Symspace already owns the GLB, which makes (a) uniquely cheap for them.
- Controllability: (a) is fully scriptable (camera path, timing, framing are deterministic); (b)–(d) rely on text prompts or coarse camera presets.
- Cost: (a) adds a Blender render (seconds–minutes CPU/GPU) before a V2V pass that costs roughly the same as an I2V pass on the same model; (d) Uni3C/Gen3C need 40–80 GB cards (not RTX 4090 class). Stable Virtual Camera is excluded by licence.
- Wan2.2-Fun-Control / VACE-Fun-A14B are the only Oct-2026 open options with depth+reference conditioning on a current-generation backbone; LTX-2.x cannot be used for depth-driven render-to-video today (only generic IC-LoRA v2v), so a hybrid (Wan for structure-locked passes, LTX for audio-bearing B-roll) is plausible.

### Gaps
- No independent, quantitative evidence that depth/edge-conditioned VACE "reliably preserves geometry" for *products with printed text*; the Blender→VACE workflow claims alignment but the page was blocked. Needs an in-house test.
- ConsID-Gen weights unavailable as of the last repo state read; no Taobao/JD/Shopee open-source product-video model found in search.
- "Diffusion as Shader", "Go-with-the-Flow", CameraCtrl II, CAT4D, ReconX, OmniGen2, VideoAlchemist, AnyV2V, ProPainter were not researched due to budget; no claims made.
- Uni3C/ReCamMaster ComfyUI VRAM figures on 24 GB cards not found (kijai supports them, but no numbers).

## KQ3. Templated (deterministic render) vs generative, and background/relighting models

### Takeaway
A deterministic Blender/Unity render with HDRI lighting and scripted camera moves is fully commercial-safe and never distorts the product; generative models are best confined to backgrounds and lighting. IC-Light (Apache-2.0, SD1.5) is the proven open relighting/background model for stills — swap its BRIA RMBG dependency for commercial use; IC-Light V2 (Flux-based, Oct 2024) carries its own licence that could not be read. Text overlays via FFmpeg/MoviePy are licence-free; Remotion requires a paid company licence above 3 employees.

### Cited Findings
- Blender headless rendering and turntable scripting as above. — [UArizona HPC](https://hpcdocs.hpc.arizona.edu/running_jobs/visualization/blender/command_line_rendering/); [blenderless](https://pypi.org/project/blenderless/0.1.7/)
- A granted US patent (US 12,381,848 B1, Aug 2025) describes exactly this: "uploading a 3D model, selecting product features, rotating the 3D model to identify features, and automatically generating 3D rendered videos for e-commerce product pages". — [Google Patents US12381848](https://patents.google.com/patent/US12381848) (title/abstract via search; possible freedom-to-operate note for the report, not legal advice)
- IC-Light (lllyasviel): Apache-2.0; models `iclight_sd15_fc` (text+foreground), `fcon` (offset noise), `fbc` (text+foreground+background); "the original BRIA RMBG 1.4 is for non-commercial use. If you use IC-Light in commercial projects, replace it with other background replacer like BiRefNet." — [IC-Light GitHub](https://github.com/lllyasviel/IC-Light)
- IC-Light V2 (released 2024-10-28): Flux-based, foreground conditioning; "The license is the IC-Light V2 License (see HF repo)". — [comfyui-wiki IC-Light V2](https://comfyui-wiki.com/en/models/ic-light/ic-light-v2) (snippet; HF licence text not readable here)
- Remotion licence: free for individuals, non-profits and "for-profit companies with up to 3 employees"; larger companies need the paid Company License. — [Remotion LICENSE.md](https://github.com/remotion-dev/remotion/blob/main/LICENSE.md)

### Inferences
- A two-tier product: Tier 1 = pure render (always succeeds, ~zero marginal GPU cost, 100% identity); Tier 2 = render + generative environment/relight (VACE/Fun-Control or IC-Light background for the hero frame followed by I2V), with automatic fallback to Tier 1 when a QA check (e.g., SSIM/DINO similarity between render and output silhouette) fails.
- For Unity-native teams, Unity Recorder/batch mode can replace Blender for the turntable; not researched here.

### Gaps
- LightLab, DiffusionLight, "Relightable", ControlNet-depth background generation and IC-Light V2's exact licence were not verified.
- No source found on Unity Recorder headless/batch rendering for this purpose.

## KQ6. Known failure modes for product videos and mitigations

### Takeaway
Reported failure modes are consistent across sources: logos/text smear or migrate, product shape and material drift over frames, extra fingers in hand-held shots, and lighting/reflection mismatch. Consensus mitigations: lock the first frame to a real/rendered image, feed multi-view references, keep the product static and move the camera, use structure conditioning, lower motion, and composite text/logos in post. These sources are practitioner blogs (secondary), not peer-reviewed; the only academic treatment found is eBay's ConsID-Gen, which frames identity drift under viewpoint change as the central e-commerce blocker.

### Cited Findings
- "Product logos can become smears or move to the wrong side"; "text and logos distort, with brand elements becoming abstract patterns"; "Text requires precise, pixel-level consistency that conflicts with AI's probabilistic generation". — [pctechmag Aug 2026](https://pctechmag.com/2026/08/from-face-drift-to-product-distortion-how-to-fix-ai-video-consistency/) (snippet); [knowlify](https://knowlify.com/articles/fix-ai-generated-video-mistakes) (snippet)
- Mitigations listed: image-stage anchor ("a human or automated check selects the correct frame, and only then does the video stage animate that locked visual anchor"); minimum reference set front/45°/side/back + logo detail + scale shot; "Add text in post-production… Overlay or crop work best for logos and small screens, while a corrected still plus image-to-video works best for contained visual errors"; short negative constraints "no logo distortion", "no extra fingers", "keep product stable". — [eachlabs 2026](https://www.eachlabs.ai/blog/how-to-orchestrate-text-to-image-to-video-workflows-in-2026) (snippet); [magichour troubleshooting](https://magichour.ai/blog/ai-video-prompt-troubleshooting) (snippet)
- ConsID-Gen: "identity preservation is a major roadblock for deploying video generation in real-world e-commerce applications"; solution is geometry-aware multi-view conditioning. — [CVPR 2026](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Wu_ConsID-Gen_View-Consistent_and_Identity-Preserving_Image-to-Video_Generation_CVPR_2026_paper.html)
- MAGREF explicitly targets "copy-paste artifacts" and "entanglement among multiple reference subjects" in reference-driven generation. — [MAGREF](https://github.com/MAGREF-Video/MAGREF)
- Hunyuan licence requires public disclosure of machine-generated content; LTX licence requires "clear disclaimers" on machine-generated content — a compliance step for client-facing marketing videos. — [Hunyuan LICENSE](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/HunyuanVideo-1.5/main/LICENSE); [LTX LICENSE-2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Lightricks/LTX-2/main/LICENSE-2)

### Inferences
- The mitigations map directly onto Strategy 2 of KQ2: a rendered turntable *is* the "locked anchor" for every frame, not just the first; depth/edge control keeps shape; logos survive because they are rendered, not generated; hands are avoided entirely unless a presenter (Wan2.2-S2V) is explicitly wanted.
- Automated QA is feasible: compare generated frames against the render (silhouette IoU, DINO/CLIP similarity, OCR of logo region) and re-roll or fall back to the pure render on failure.

### Gaps
- No open-source "logo/text restoration for video" tool was found; face-restoration analogues (GFPGAN/CodeFormer) do not apply to logos. Material (glossy vs matte) errors are mentioned anecdotally only; no quantitative study found.
- Blocked pages (pctechmag, magichour, morphic, genra, eachlabs) were only read via search snippets.

## Recommended pipeline (synthesis for the report writer; inference, not a cited finding)

1. **Deterministic base (always produced):** Blender headless (`blender -b … --python turntable.py`) imports the GLB, applies an HDRI, renders a 5–10 s orbit plus depth and outline passes at 1280×720/24 fps; FFmpeg adds brand text and an ACE-Step music bed (Apache-2.0). Licence-clean, identity-perfect, ~$0.01/clip.
2. **Generative "unique" variant:** Wan2.2-VACE-Fun-A14B or Wan2.2-Fun-Control (Apache-2.0) in headless ComfyUI (kijai wrapper, FP8/GGUF on 24 GB or BF16 on H100) with the depth+canny passes as control and the hero render as reference; prompt describes the lifestyle environment. Optional: IC-Light (Apache-2.0, with BiRefNet) to relight the hero still first. QA check vs render; fall back to step 1.
3. **Audio-bearing B-roll / social cut:** LTX-2.5 or 2.3 I2V from the hero render (free while Symspace revenue < US$10M; paid licence above) for 1080p–4K clips with native audio, ~3 min on a 4090; or Wan2.2-S2V-14B + Kokoro/Chatterbox for a presenter.
4. **Avoid for Symspace unless the geography is confirmed:** HunyuanVideo 1.5 / HunyuanCustom / HunyuanVideo-Foley (EU/UK/KR excluded), MiniMax H3 (reported US/EU/UK/KR local-deployment block), Stable Virtual Camera (non-commercial), MMAudio / F5-TTS / Fish Speech weights (non-commercial).
